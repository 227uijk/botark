# Getting Help with BotArk

Need help installing, signing into an account, or configuring proxies in **BotArk**?

## 1. Quick Guides & FAQ

- **English Guide & FAQ**: [README.md](README.md)
- **简体中文使用指南与常见问题**: [docs/readme/README_zh.md](docs/readme/README_zh.md)

## 2. Where to Ask Questions

- **Usage Questions & Workflow Sharing**: Open a thread in [**GitHub Discussions**](https://github.com/qybaihe/botark/discussions).
- **Bug Reports & Feature Requests**: Open an issue in [**GitHub Issues**](https://github.com/qybaihe/botark/issues/new/choose).
- **Security Vulnerabilities**: Report privately via [**Security Advisories**](SECURITY.md) — never paste real tokens or cookies into public issues.
